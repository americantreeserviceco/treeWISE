## 5. Success rule for the first cycle

- **Keep a ZIP** if it produces at least one booking at a cost per booking the owner accepts.
- **Pause a ZIP** that produces no bookings after the budget for that ZIP is spent, and move its budget to the next ZIP in the playbook ranking.
- **Review all ZIPs at the end** of the test. Compare the top-median Denver ZIPs against the Golden ZIPs.

The owner sets the spend cap and the acceptable cost per booking before launch.
